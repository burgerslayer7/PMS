# Attribution and third-party notices

Pokémon Mount System is a clean-room implementation. No source code from
Dramatic Sky Ride is included.

## Bundled PokéPC walker sheets

PMS bundles the numbered 16×96 walker sheets `follower_001.png` through
`follower_251.png` from the authorized fork:

- Project: PokéPC Followers
- Repository: <https://github.com/burgerslayer7/PokePCFollowers>
- Source release: v0.8.3
- Source commit: `78c80f61e166e67efd92d929207caa92e896e789`
- Imported files: the 251 numbered walker sheets only

The source fork credits the Generation I and II overworld/follower art to the
PokéPC / Followers EX lineage, ShockSlayer, and the Pokémon Crystal Clear team.
Its Generation II sheets were distributed through the built-in Poke Followers
/ GSC pack in Wilds of Kanto.

Wilds of Kanto's third-party notice further preserves credit for TRW, DAX,
Antigravity and the other authors named by the upstream follower projects.

These images remain subject to the rights and attribution requirements of
their original authors and sources. They are not relicensed under the Wilds of
Kanto MIT license, the PokéPC project, or Pokémon Mount System. No Pokémon ROM,
Crystal 251 ROM content, Stadium model, or Stadium 2 model is bundled.

Machine-readable provenance and the audited aggregate checksum are in
`assets/fallback/pokepc/SOURCE.json`.
